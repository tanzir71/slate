Ownership

Your files. Your folders.
Forever.

  • Plain .md is the source of truth

    Every sheet is a normal Markdown file. Open it in another editor, keep it in Git, or come back in a decade.

  • A folder, or your browser

    Work directly in a folder in Chrome or Edge. Keep a private browser library in Firefox and Safari.

  • Conflicts become copies

    When edits disagree, Slate keeps both versions. Your words deserve a second copy, not a replacement.

  • Take everything with you

    Export your whole Space as a .zip. Markdown, assets, and your recoverable trash travel together.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Slate really free?

Yes. Writing on your own files is free, with no account. Optional encrypted Slate Sync is planned last, and will be free while in beta.

Where are my files?

In a folder you choose, or in your browser’s private storage. Export the browser library as a .zip whenever you want a portable copy.

Can I open my Obsidian vault?

Yes. Open the vault’s folder in Chrome or Edge, or import its Markdown files into a browser library. Your original files stay plain text.

Does Slate use AI?

No AI service is required. Your writing stays on your device. You can write offline, and no account stands between you and your words.
